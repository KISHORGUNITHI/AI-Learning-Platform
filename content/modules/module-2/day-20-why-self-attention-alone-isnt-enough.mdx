## What Does Attention Actually Do?

Self-Attention answers only one question:

> **Which tokens should influence me?**

It never asks:

> **How should I transform this representation?**

## One Simple Analogy

## Key Takeaways

- Self-Attention exchanges information between tokens.
- It does not deeply process that information.
- Another neural network is required — the Feed Forward Network.
- Every Transformer Block alternates: **communicate then transform**.
