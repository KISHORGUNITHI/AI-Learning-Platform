## Quick Recap

After Multi-Head Attention, GPT has 12 separate head outputs — each `(3 × 64)`. But the next Transformer layer expects `(3 × 768)`.

How are these combined?

## Engineering Insight

Concatenation simply places all head outputs side by side along the feature dimension. WO then learns how to **mix** information across all heads into one coherent representation — this is where multi-head knowledge is integrated.

## Key Takeaways

- Every head produces its own `(3 × 64)` contextual representation.
- Concatenation combines all outputs into `(3 × 768)`.
- The embedding dimension is restored to 768.
- WO mixes information across all heads.
- The final output is passed to the next Transformer layer.
