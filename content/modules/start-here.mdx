> *"Don't aim to memorize AI. Aim to understand it."*

Welcome!

I'm glad you're here.

Before we dive into Transformers, Large Language Models, RAG, or AI Agents, I want to spend a few minutes explaining **why this platform exists** and **how we'll learn together**.

This isn't just another collection of AI tutorials. It's a carefully structured journey where every concept builds naturally on the previous one.

---

## The Problem

Learning AI today can be confusing.

One tutorial explains **Transformers**.

Another explains **RAG**.

Another teaches **AI Agents**.

Each topic is interesting on its own, but they often feel disconnected.

After watching dozens of videos, many learners know the terminology but still wonder:

> **"How does all of this fit together?"**

That's the problem this platform aims to solve.

Instead of teaching isolated concepts, we'll build one complete understanding of modern AI.

---

## Our Learning Philosophy

Every lesson in this roadmap follows a simple pattern.

```text
Problem
↓
Intuition
↓
Concept
↓
Internal Working
↓
Worked Example
↓
Connections
↓
Key Takeaways
```

Rather than asking you to memorize definitions, we'll first understand **why a concept exists**, then explore **how it works**, and finally see **where it fits into the bigger picture**.

By the end of the roadmap, AI should feel like one connected system—not a collection of unrelated buzzwords.

---

## Before You Begin

As you move through this roadmap, try to keep asking three simple questions:

- **Why does this exist?**
- **What problem does it solve?**
- **How does it connect to everything else?**

Those questions will help you build a deeper understanding than simply memorizing definitions.

---

## Key Takeaways

- This roadmap teaches AI as one connected journey.
- Every lesson builds on previous concepts.
- We'll focus on understanding **why** before **how**.
- You don't need prior AI knowledge to get started.
